Privacy Policy

App: WA Heavy Vehicle Licence Prep

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Overview

WA Heavy Vehicle Licence Prep is an educational app designed to help users practise heavy vehicle licence questions in Western Australia.

Information We Collect

The app does not require an account or registration. The app developer does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location information, or other personal information through the app.

Quiz Progress

Quiz answers, scores, and progress may be stored locally on your device to support the app's functionality. This information is not sent to the app developer or shared with third parties by the app.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app does not display advertising and does not intentionally integrate third-party analytics or tracking services. Standard operating system and app distribution services may process technical information under their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

The app developer does not sell or share personal information collected through the app.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for people studying for heavy vehicle licences and is not specifically directed at children.

Security and Data Retention

Any locally stored quiz progress remains on your device and may be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to device backup settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer using the contact details provided on the app's Google Play Store listing.

Disclaimer

This is an independent educational application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Western Australia or its licensing authorities.
